# LABCOST – Learning and Business Cost Simulator

**LABCOST** is an interactive web-based simulator designed to support teaching and learning in **cost accounting**, **managerial accounting**, and **decision making**.

The system allows students and managers to simulate and analyze:

- Contribution Margin (unit & total)  
- Break-even point (units & revenue)  
- Operating Income (Profit)  
- Degree of Operating Leverage (DOL)  
- Revenue, Variable Cost, and Profit behavior across different sales levels  

LABCOST is ideal for:

- Cost Accounting classes  
- Managerial Accounting (Controllership)  
- MBA programs  
- Executive training  
- Financial literacy workshops
